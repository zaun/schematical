# SN54HC161, SN74HC161 4-bit synchronous binary counters

## Description

Synchronous, presettable 4-bit binary counters with internal carry
look-ahead for fast cascading. All four flip-flops are clocked together
on the rising edge of a buffered clock (CLK), so the outputs change
together, without the spikes of ripple counters.

Loading is synchronous: a low on !{LOAD} disables counting, and the next
rising clock edge loads A–D, whatever the enable inputs. Clearing is
asynchronous: a low on !{CLR} sets all four outputs low, whatever CLK,
!{LOAD} and the enables.

ENP and ENT must both be high to count. ENT is fed forward to enable
the ripple-carry output RCO, which is high while the count is 15 and
ENT is high; RCO enables the next stage of a cascade. Changes at ENP,
ENT or !{LOAD} have no effect until the next clock edge; transitions at
ENP and ENT are allowed at any level of CLK.

SN74HC161 is specified from –40 °C to 85 °C, SN54HC161 from –55 °C to
125 °C.

## Pin functions

```pins
CLR: Asynchronous clear, active low
CLK: Clock, counts and loads on the rising edge
A, B, C, D: Preset data inputs (A is the least significant bit)
ENP: Count enable (parallel)
ENT: Count enable (trickle); also enables RCO
LOAD: Synchronous load, active low
QA, QB, QC, QD: Counter outputs (QA is the least significant bit)
RCO: Ripple-carry output, high at count 15 while ENT is high
GND: Ground
VCC: Positive supply
NC: Not internally connected
```

## Application information

### n-bit synchronous counters

Cascade stages by driving every stage's CLK from one clock, every
stage's ENP from the count-enable signal, and each stage's ENT from the
previous stage's RCO (the first stage's ENT from count enable). The
look-ahead carry supports any count mode (modulo-N, N1-to-N2,
N1-to-maximum).

With three or more stages this circuit has a limit. RCO is the AND of
ENT, QA, QB, QC and QD, and in the second and later stages QA rises
before ENT falls, so RCO glitches high for 7–12 ns (t~g~) when the
earlier stage rolls over. If the glitch is still present at the next
rising clock edge, the following stage counts wrongly. So for more than
two cascaded parts:

```equation
f~max~ = 1 / (t~pd~(CLK to RCO) + t~g~)
```

At 25 °C and V~CC~ = 4.5 V this is 1 / (43 ns + 12 ns) ≈ 18 MHz. One
part, or two cascaded, are not limited by the glitch. A glitch can also
appear on RCO of a single part, depending on the timing of ENT to CLK:
any use of RCO other than driving the ENT of another '161 must allow
for it.
