## 3. Design

### 3.1 Surface-mount, radial and axial fuses

As plan-47-D § 3: ids `FUSE-<LLWW>-<MAKER>`, `FUSE-RAD-<L>-<P>-<MAKER>`
(a box fuse: body length, lead spacing), `FUSE-AX-<L>-<P>-<MAKER>`; rows in
`dims-E.tsv`, generated by `fp-gen.py`; reuse and shared ids by
`fp-compare.py`. An axial fuse's span is R7's (`docs/parts-format.md`) from
the body length; no pin-1 mark. A square-end-block or J-lead fuse uses the
datasheet's recommended pad layout (R1).

### 3.2 The clip pairs

A cartridge fuse's footprint is two clips of plan-47-A § 6's part for its
maker and size, facing each other on the fuse's axis: ids
`FUSE-CLIP-5X20-<MAKER>` and `FUSE-CLIP-6X32-<MAKER>` (`LITTELFUSE`,
`EATON`, `SCHURTER`), style `explicit`, `pins` 2:

- each clip's holes are the clip datasheet's PCB pattern (hole positions
  and diameter; `drill` the datasheet's hole, the round pad by R5); every
  leg of the clip at end 1 is a pad of pin 1, every leg at end 2 a pad of
  pin 2;
- the distance between the two clips is the clip datasheet's own two-clip
  mounting pattern for the fuse size (§ 2 Verified: all five datasheets
  print one);
- `body` is the fuse in its clips: the pair's overall length × the clip
  width;
- `note`: the clip part number, its PDF under `datasheets/fuse/` and page.

Map rows: every `Requires Holder` line of a maker and size names that
maker's pair (the 9 lines map to 6 ids).

Where the risk concentrates: reading the two-clip pattern for the right
fuse length (the Littelfuse and Eaton sheets cover several clip types and
fuse sizes on one page) and the SMD square-end-block fuses' pad layout. One hand
check per clip pair against the fuse's length, and per SMD series.

Rejected alternatives: (1) one maker-neutral clip pair per size (rejected:
the library's footprints follow a maker's drawing, and the three makers'
clips have different hole patterns); (2) a fuse-holder footprint (rejected:
a holder is a second BOM part with its own range of footprints; the owner
asked for clips).

## Decisions

- **A cartridge fuse's footprint is its maker's clip pair** (plan-47-A § 6;
  § 3.2 rejected alternatives).
- **The clip spacing and holes are the clip datasheet's two-clip mounting
  pattern** (§ 3.2).
- **Leaded cartridge fuses ("5mm x 20mm (Axial)") are axial parts** (R7),
  not clip parts.
