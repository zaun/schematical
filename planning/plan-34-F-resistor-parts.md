Effort: large (3h–1d)

F writes `schematical/schematical/Parts/resistor.json`: `GENERIC-RESISTOR`
and one part per `add` row of `planning/plan-34-data/candidates.tsv`,

`resistor`; `ctest` passes; each golden grows by exactly the new parts or

## 3. Design

### 3.1 Devices

One device per (category, composition, pinout, ohms group), so the drawer
leaf (`function`) is the ohms group:

- `id`: `<category>-<composition>-<pinout>-<group>` in kebab case
  (`chip-thick-film-two-pin-1k-5k`, `axial-metal-film-two-pin-lt-1k`,
  `array-thick-film-iso-8-10k-50k`).
- `function`: the group label (`< 1 kΩ`, `1 kΩ – 5 kΩ`, …, `≥ 1 MΩ`;
  plan-34-A § Decisions).
- `drawer`: `["Resistors", "Chip"]`, `["Resistors", "Through-hole"]`,
  `["Resistors", "Array"]`.
- `description`: `<Composition> chip resistor` / `<Composition> axial
  resistor` / `Resistor array, <n> <isolated|bussed>`; a jumper's device is
  its own (`chip-jumper-two-pin-lt-1k`, `Zero-ohm jumper`).
- `pinouts`: `two-pin` (pins `1`, `2`, `inout`), or the array's pinout from
  `specs.tsv` (`iso-8`: pins 1–8 `inout`); `resistors`: `[{"a":"1","b":"2"}]`
  or the array's pairs.

### 3.2 Parts

`{"name": <mpn>, "manufacturer": <maker>, "package": <specs.tsv package>,
"pinout": <pinout>, "footprint": <specs.tsv footprint>, "datasheet": <id>,
"ohms": <ohms>, "tolerance": <1 | 5, absent for a jumper>, "power":
<watts per element from DigiKey's Power column, first number>, "flags":
["military"] when the row is flagged}`. Datasheet ids:
`<maker-slug>-<PDF stem, lowercased, non-alphanumerics → ->`
(`yageo-pyu-rc-group-51-rohs-l`), one `datasheets.json` entry per PDF with
its URL from `datasheets/resistor/datasheets.tsv`.

### 3.3 GENERIC-RESISTOR

Device `resistor-generic`, function `Generic`, drawer `["Resistors"]`,
description `Generic resistor`, pinout `two-pin`, resistors
`[{"a":"1","b":"2"}]`. Part: `{"name": "GENERIC-RESISTOR", "manufacturer":
"Generic", "package": "Axial", "pinout": "two-pin", "footprint":
"RES-AXIAL-10.16", "footprintChoices": [0402 RES-0402, 0603 RES-0603, 0805
RES-0805, 1206 RES-1206, Axial RES-AXIAL-10.16, Radial RES-RADIAL-2.54],
"ohms": 1000}` (the pulls' six choices; Axial and 1 kΩ by default, the
owner's decision). No tolerance, no power, no datasheet.

## Decisions

- **One device per (category, composition, pinout, group)** — author: the
  drawer leaf is the device's `function`, so the ohms group must be a
  device field; composition keeps the description true.
- **`Generic` as a leaf beside the subcategories** — author: the drawer
  category holds both functions and subcategories
  (`PartCatalog.swift:893–895`, `:1083`); plan-33 puts `GENERIC-CAPACITOR`
  the same way.
- **Power: the first number of DigiKey's column** — author: `0.1W, 1/10W`
  is one value written twice.
- Owner decisions: plan-34-A § Decisions.
