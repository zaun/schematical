## Supply and electrical

- VDD (pin 10) 3-6 V "voltage regulator programmed to 4.5 V at 25 C"; abs. max 8 V (pcf1179c p.11 Table 6, p.12 Table 7). VDD is the
  chip's own **shunt-regulated** rail: the application feeds it from the 12 V battery through an external resistor R
  ((12 V - 5 V) / 700 uA = 10 k max, typical 7 k at 1000 uA; IDD 700 uA min, 950 uA typ, 3 mA abs. max) with an external 47 uF
  capacitor Cext (p.12 Table 7 + note 1, p.13 Fig 8). `vcc` [3, 6] and `inputMax` 6.3 (VI max VDD + 0.3, p.11); the regulator, its EEPROM
  programming (VPP, DATA, SEL, p.8-9) and the 0.35 %/K temperature compensation (p.6-7 Fig 6) are **not modeled**: a supply rail on VDD
  stands in for the regulated output.
- Inputs: S1, S2, TS, SEL, DATA, VPP have an internal pull-up (45 / 90 / 180 k, p.12 Table 7; VPP p.8); MODE a pull-up/pull-down 100 / 300 / 1000 k;
  ENABLE and FLASH none (leakage 2 uA).
- Backplane output resistance <= 3 k, segment <= 5 k (p.12). Outputs are three-level (VDD, VDD/2, 0; p.5-6 Fig 5); the engine is binary
  (the `lcd` driver's 0/1 AC drive stands in; no bias levels).
- Oscillator: crystal 4.194304 MHz (Rs 50, CL 12 pF), OSC_IN pin 3 / OSC_OUT pin 4 (analog, p.12 Table 7). Calibration (EEPROM, 0-31 inhibited
  262 144 Hz pulses per second, backplane period 15.625-15.861 ms, p.9-10 Table 5) not modeled; the nominal n = 0 value is used.

## Behavior (all from pcf1179c p.7-8)

- Duplex (1:2) drive: BP1 and BP2, frame (one period of both) **15.625 ms** (n = 0) = 4096 periods of 262 144 Hz; BP1 sequence per frame
  0, VDD, VDD/2, VDD/2 and BP2 VDD/2, VDD/2, 0, VDD (p.6 Fig 5). So `frameUs` 15625, a slot 7812.5 us.
- Time: hours, minutes and seconds counters from the 4.19 MHz crystal (1 Hz seconds). The seconds are not displayed; they only carry into
  minutes. 12-hour or 24-hour (MODE), AM/PM annunciator in 12-hour mode (p.5 Fig 3: "PM 2:34" in 12-hour, "14:34" with no AM/PM in 24-hour).
- Hour-tens digit 1: blank for hours 0-9, "1" (B1, C1) for 10-19, "2" (A1, B1, D1, E1, G1) for 20-23. The sheet shows only the "1" (Fig 3); the
  "2" shape is the only one the glass wiring (B1/C1 separate, the rest tied) can make: inference, flagged. 12-hour mode hours 10-12 show "1".
- Power-on (p.7 s.7.4): MODE = VDD: 12-hour mode, **1:00 AM**; MODE = VSS: 24-hour, **0:00**; MODE open: 24-hour, 0:00 or 1:00 (not
  determined by the sheet). MODE open and a reset: the mode switches 12 <-> 24 hour (s.7.3).
- Colon (s.7.5): FLASH = VDD: the colon pulses at 1 Hz; FLASH = VSS: static (on). The duty cycle is not given; 50 % assumed (colon on while the
  second is odd, or even: not given either: flagged).
- Time setting (s.7.6-7.9): S1 and S2 have pull-ups and a debounce circuit (30 / 65 / 100 ms min/typ/max, p.12 Table 7). Enabled only while
  ENABLE is at VDD (s.7.7).
  - S1 to VSS: the displayed hour advances by one; after one second it continues with 4 advances per second until S1 is released.
  - S2 to VSS: the minutes advance by one, then (after one second) 4 per second until released; **each** minute advance also resets the
    seconds counter to zero; a minute overflow (59 to 0) does **not** carry into the hours.
  - 12-hour: the hour advance walks the display through 1-12 and flips AM/PM when passing 11 to 12 (inference: the sheet says only "advances by one"). The sheet never says whether 12-hour shows 12:00 or 0:00 after 11:59; the 12 convention of every 12-hour clock (and of `rtc` `h12`) is assumed.
- Segment test / reset (s.7.10): S1 and S2 both at VSS: **all LCD segments ON**. Releasing the buttons "resets the display"; no reset occurs
  when DATA is at VSS. The reset is taken to be the power-on state (s.7.3: a reset re-evaluates MODE; s.7.4: start-up 1:00 AM / 0:00): inference, flagged.
- TS at VSS (s.7.11): all counters (seconds, minutes, hours) stop; debounce and auto-increment times 64 x faster.
- Not modeled: voltage regulator, LCD voltage / temperature coefficient, EEPROM programming and time calibration (VPP, DATA pulses, SEL), oscillator
  start time (200 ms max), bias levels, power consumption.

## Can the `rtc` `clock` section drive what an `lcd` driver displays? **No.** Exactly why

`docs/parts-format.md` § `rtc` / § `leddriver`, `converter_load.c`, `rtc.c`:

1. **Kind exclusion.** `converter_load.c` (about lines 3103-3112) loads `clock` only for an `rtc` device and `display` only for a
   `leddriver`/`lcddriver` device (an `else if` chain); `clock` is rejected on `lcddriver` as `unknown key`, `display` on `rtc`. So one device cannot have
   both a counting clock and an `lcd` section.
2. **No bus.** `load_bus` (`converter_load.c` 1182-1195) requires a `"bus"` object with a `style` (`spi`, `i2c`, `i2c-spi`, `parallel`, `clocked`).
   The PCF1179C has no host bus; its registers are internal.
3. **What `clock` can do, were it allowed:** `clock.fields` maps `second`/`minute`/`hour` counters to register fields (BCD), the counters advance
   once per simulated second (`rtc.c` `tick_ps`, 1e12 ps), 12-hour mode comes from a field (`h12`, `h12Level`) with a PM bit (`pm`), `pins.levels`
   copies a pin's level into a field (MODE to the h12 field; Z reads 0, so MODE open is 24-hour, the open-circuit toggle is not modeled),
   `stop` halts counting (TS = VSS would use it via `pins.levels`).
4. **What it cannot do:** (a) the power-on time is always 2000-01-01 00:00:00 (`docs` § `rtc`): 12-hour mode would start at 12:00 AM, not 1:00 AM;
   (b) time changes only when the *bus* writes a time register, which reloads the counters (`load()`); there is no pin-driven increment, no debounce
   and no auto-repeat, and no action "reset seconds"; (c) no reset-to-start-up action; (d) no derived colon bit.
5. **The `lcd` driver has no decode.** Its `ram` is a bit stream whose bits are segment on/off values (`ramOrder`, `bitOrder`); a BCD hour field cannot
   be turned into the segments of a digit, and the hour-tens "1"/"2"/blank table needs a decode as well. There is no `test` key (all segments on) either.

So even the display side alone ("registers HOURS/MINUTES decoded to the segment outputs") cannot be written in the format today (points 2 and 5): there is nothing that writes the
registers and nothing that decodes them. **No device was written** (a half model would be a fake). `newDevices` in the result file is empty.

## Engine needs for a faithful PCF1179C (exact semantics)

An `lcddriver` device `PCF1179C` with these additions; keys named for the main thread, all new:

1. `bus`: `{"style": "none"}`: allowed on `leddriver`/`lcddriver` devices; no bus pins, `protocol` optional; the registers are written only by the clock, the buttons and reset.
2. `clock` allowed in `lcddriver` (and `leddriver`) next to `display` (the `rtc.c` engine run for those kinds), with the fields below; plus
   `clock.powerOn`: `{"hour": {"field": "CFG.H12", "values": {"0": 0, "1": 1}}, "minute": 0, "second": 0}`: the counters' value at power-on and after a reset (here 0:00 in 24-hour, 1:00 in 12-hour).
3. `display.lcd` **decode** table: `"decode": [{"field": "REG.FIELD", "cells": [{"pin": "S4", "bp": 0}, ...], "table": ["1111110", ...]}]`:
   for the field's value v, cell i of the list is lit when `table[v][i]` is `1` (a value past the table: all off). A cell is lit when its RAM bit is 1 **or** any decode lights it.
   Several decode cells may name the same pin and bp (A1/D1/E1/G1, A3/D3). `field` value can be a BCD digit field or a derived field (below).
4. `display.lcd` **`test`**: a condition; while true every segment pin is driven at the opposite of the frame level in every slot (every cell lit), as `digits.test`.
5. No new derived field kind is needed: the BCD register fields the `clock` writes can be split (`HOUR.TENS` bits 5-4, `HOUR.UNITS` bits 3-0, `MIN.TENS`, `MIN.UNITS`), so the decode tables above read them directly. The
   hour-tens table has 3 entries (0 blank, 1 = B1 + C1, 2 = A1 B1 D1 E1 G1); the units digits use the 0-9 glyph table of the SN74143 section below (the standard 7-segment shapes: Fig 3 shows only 1, 2, 3, 4, so the 6/9/7 forms are assumed); the minute-tens digit uses 0-5. The
   colon flash needs only `SEC.UNITS` bit 0 (the BCD seconds-units bit 0 alternates exactly each second, 59 to 00 included), named as a one-bit field `SEC.ODD` in `fields`.
6. `display.lcd` **conditional cells**: `"cells": [{"pin": "S7", "bp": 1, "when": ["CFG.FLASH=0"]}, {"pin": "S7", "bp": 1, "when": ["CFG.FLASH=1", "SEC.ODD=1"]}, {"pin": "S13", "bp": 0, "when": ["CFG.H12=1", "HOUR.PM=0"]}, ...]`: a cell (pin, bp) lit while its `when` conditions hold (several entries on the same cell = OR). Colon (FLASH): on static, or on in the odd seconds while FLASH = VDD; AM (pin 26, BP1): 12-hour and `HOUR.PM` = 0; PM (pin 26, BP2): 12-hour and `HOUR.PM` = 1.
7. `buttons` section (the S1/S2 state machine), run on the simulated clock:
   ```json
   "buttons": {"enable": "ENABLE=1", "fast": "TS=0",
               "debounceMs": 65, "firstRepeatMs": 1000, "repeatPerSec": 4, "fastDivisor": 64,
               "keys": [{"pin": "S1", "active": 0, "increment": "HOUR", "resetSeconds": false},
                        {"pin": "S2", "active": 0, "increment": "MIN", "resetSeconds": true, "carry": false}],
               "both": {"test": "TEST", "reset": {"action": "reset", "unless": "DATA=0"}}}
   ```
   Semantics: a key counts as pressed after its pin has been at `active` for `debounceMs` (30-100, typical 65, p.12) while `enable` holds; at that moment the named counter
   advances by one (hours mod 24, minutes mod 60 **without** carrying into hours) and, for S2, the seconds counter is set to 0; if the key is still pressed `firstRepeatMs` (1000 ms, s.7.8) later, it advances every 1/`repeatPerSec` s (250 ms) until released; while the `fast` pin is at 0
   (TS) the debounce, first-repeat and repeat times are divided by 64 (s.7.11) and counting (the 1 Hz tick) is stopped. While both keys are pressed (both past debounce) the `both.test` bit is 1 (the `lcd.test` condition) and no increment happens (inference: the sheet only says all segments are ON); releasing both while `DATA` is not low runs the reset (counters to `powerOn`, mode re-read from MODE).
   The 64 x TS fast path: debounce 65/64 ms, first repeat 15.6 ms, repeat 3.9 ms.
8. A bundled glass (`lcd` kind, not yet in `lcd.json`: the four `GENERIC-LCD-*` glasses have no colon and no AM/PM): `GENERIC-LCD-CLOCK-1:2`, 28 pins named as the chip's segment pins, 2 backplanes, cells = the table in "Pins" (digit 1: A1 D1 E1 G1 on pin 25/BP2, B1 pin 24/BP1, C1 pin 24/BP2, no F1; colon `L1`; the annunciators need two **new glass segment names** `AM` and `PM`: `docs/parts-format.md` § `lcd` lists only A-G, DP, L1-L3, LB, MINUS).

Registers the device would have: `SEC` (SECONDS 7 bit BCD), `MIN`, `HOUR` (HOURS, `H12` strap, `PM`), `CFG` (`H12` from MODE via `pins.levels`, `FLASH` from FLASH via `pins.levels`, `TEST` internal). `display`: `{"driver": "lcd", "mode": "1:2", "backplanes": ["BP1", "BP2"], "segmentPins": [pins 14-26], "frameUs": 15625}`.

## Behavior (sn74143 p.1-2, p.7 function table, p.3 logic diagram)

Block: a 4-stage synchronous **decade** BCD counter (0-9, then 0), a 4-bit transparent latch after it, a BCD to 7-segment decoder reading the **latch**, the segment drivers (and DP), and the blanking logic.

- **CLR** low: resets the counter to 0 and holds it (asynchronous); must be high to count (p.2; width 25 ns min, clear inactive set-up 60 ns, p.4).
- **CLK**: each positive-going transition increments the counter provided SCEI is low, PCEI is low and CLR is high (p.2). The enables must not change while the clock is low (a rule of use; both must meet set-up 30 ns before the rising edge, p.4): the model samples them at the rising edge. Max clock 18 MHz typ (12 min), clock high 25 ns, low 55 ns (p.4-5).
- **SCEI** high: counter inhibited and MAX driven high. **PCEI** high: counter inhibited (and MAX unaffected: it depends on SCEI only).
- **MAX** (pin 22, push-pull logic output, I_OL 11.2 mA): low when the **counter** (not the latch) is at 9 and SCEI is low; high at counts 0-8 and whenever SCEI is high (p.2; function table: MAX = L only in the count-9 rows; derived from the stage outputs, p.3).
- **STRB** (latch strobe): low, the latches follow the counter (transparent); high, the latches hold and the counter counts on independently (p.2). The function table (p.7) shows it: strobe goes high with the count at 5, the latch stays 5 (`QD QC QB QA` = L H L H, display "5") through counts 6 and 7, and when strobe falls the latch follows the counter again (8, then 9).
- **QA-QD** (pins 17-20): the **latched** BCD value, weights 1, 2, 4, 8 (push-pull logic outputs, "active pull-ups", I_OH -240 uA, I_OL 4.8 mA); they and the segments both come from the latch.
- **Segments a-g**: the decode of the latched BCD digit; glyphs below. Outputs are **constant-current sinks**: on = the pin sinks about 15 mA (a-g) / 7 mA (dp) over an output voltage of 1-5 V; off = off-state current <= 250 uA at up to 7 V (p.1, p.4-5 Table: I_O(on) 9 / 15 / 22 mA for a-g at VO 1 V / 5 V; dp 4.5 / 7 / 12 mA; V_O(off) 7 V min). They drive **common-anode** LEDs (TIL302/303) "without series resistors"; "any number of LEDs in series may be driven as long as the output voltage rating is not exceeded" (p.1). So lit = pin low (current into the pin), off = high impedance, **no pull-up**: the 7 V off-state rating on a 5 V part (p.5) means there is no pull-up transistor on those pins (the page-4 "typical of all outputs" schematic is the totem-pole of the logic outputs: inference, flagged). The 15 mA constant-current behavior is **not modeled** (the engine has no current).
- **Glyphs** (a b c d e f g; read from p.2 and the p.7 table, `ON` columns; both agree):

  | code | a b c d e f g | | code | a b c d e f g |

  `glyphs`: `["1111110","0110000","1101101","1111001","0110011","1011011","1011111","1110000","1111111","1111011"]` (same as the 4026B). No glyph for 10-15 (the counter never leaves 0-9).
- **DP** (pin 7): the decimal point driver (pin 8) lights while DP is high (p.2: "must be high to display decimal point"); it is **not** displayed when DP is low or the display is blanked. Its own current is 7 mA nominal.
- **BI** (pin 5) high: blanks the entire display (every segment and dp off) and forces RBO low (p.2, note D); must be low for normal display. Counting, latch, MAX and Q are not affected.
- **RBI** (pin 4) low with the **latched** value 0: blanks the whole display (dp too, p.7 row "Ripple Blank"/"Clear" shows dp OFF) and forces RBO low (p.2; note E). No effect when the latch is not 0. RBI must be open or high to show a zero (note C).
- **RBO** (pin 6): a low when BI is high or (RBI is low and the latch is 0); otherwise high. Open collector with a resistive pull-up (about 10 k, p.4) for wire-AND; as an input, a **low on this pin blanks the whole display** (p.2: "may be used as an active-low blanking input"; note A: wire-AND). I_OL 4.8 mA, I_OH -120 uA.
- Power-on: the counter and latch are not defined by the sheet (the engine's 0 convention applies: display shows 0, MAX high, RBO high unless RBI is low).
- Priority: blank (BI high, or RBO pulled low) > ripple blank (RBI low and latch 0) > the glyph. `dp` follows `DP` only while none of these blank.

New keys for Model `seg7` (the loader adds them to `SEG7_KEYS` in `counter_div.c`, to `DEVICE_KEYS` in `counter.c`, to the Part Editor's counter `seg7` schema (`EXTRA` list / `KindSchema`, `PartKindKeysTests`), to `docs/parts-format.md`, and `counter_pin_open_drain` becomes a per-model hook), all optional and absent on every existing part so every existing golden line is unchanged:

1. **`"segmentsActive": "high" | "low"`**, default `"high"`: the pin level of a lit segment (and of the dp output). `"low"` drives 0 for lit and 1 (or Z, key 2) for off. As decoder `seg7`'s key of the same name (SN7447A: low).
2. **`"segmentsOutput": "push-pull" | "open-drain"`**, default `"push-pull"`: `"open-drain"`: the seven segment pins, the `decimalPoint.out` pin and the `rbo` pin drive 0 (lit / blanking) or release to Z, never 1 (the model's `seq_drive(..., SEQ_OPEN_DRAIN, ...)`); `counter_pin_open_drain` returns 1 for exactly those pins so the voltage check treats them as carrying no voltage. The Q and MAX pins stay push-pull (the sheet: "logic outputs, except RBO, have active pull-ups", p.1). Needs `segmentsActive: "low"` when open-drain (a high lit level with Z-off is rejected at load: "open-drain needs segmentsActive low").
3. **`"toggleEnable"` as an array of controls** (back-compatible: a single object stays valid): every control asserted at the clock's rising edge for the counter to step (here `[{"pin": "SCEI", "active": "low"}, {"pin": "PCEI", "active": "low"}]`). An X on any enable at an edge makes the count unknown, as the existing single `toggleEnable` does.
4. **`"maxCount": {"pin": "MAX", "enable": {"pin": "SCEI", "active": "low"}}`**: an output pin (push-pull) low while the **count** (the real counter, not the latched `shown`) is 9 and `enable` is asserted, high otherwise (count 0-8, or `enable` deasserted); X while the count is unknown or `enable` is X. Updates on the clock edge and on `enable` changes.
5. **`"latchOutputs": ["QA", "QB", "QC", "QD"]`**: 4 push-pull output pins, LSB first, driven with the bits of the **displayed (latched) count** `shown` (so with `latchEnable` high they hold; an unknown `shown` gives X).
6. **`"blank": {"pin": "BI", "active": "high"}`**: a control (as `lampTest`): while asserted every segment pin and the dp output are off and the `rbo` pin is pulled low; X blanks to X for the segments it could change (merge as in the decoder `seg7`).
7. **`"rbi": "RBI"`, `"rbo": "BI/RBO"`**, together (as the decoder `seg7`, key names identical; the `rbo` pin must have role `inout`): RBI is read active low; the **latched** code 0 and RBI low blank every segment and the dp output; the `rbo` pin is pulled low (open drain) while the `blank` control is asserted **or** (RBI low and the latched code is 0), else released; read as an input, a low on the `rbo` pin (external) blanks every segment and the dp output (Z reads high; X reads X). These replace `rippleBlank` for this part; `rippleBlank` is untouched.
8. **`"decimalPoint": {"in": "DP", "out": "dp"}`**: the `out` pin is lit (per `segmentsActive`, open-drain with key 2) while the `in` pin reads 1 and no blank (BI, RBI-with-code-0, `rbo` pulled low) holds; X while `in` is X and not blanked.

Priority of the segments (existing rule extended): blank (`blank`, or `rbo` pin low) > ripple blank (`rbi` low, latched code 0) > the glyph; `lampTest` is absent on the part (not on the sheet).

Optional timing extras (`SEG7_EXTRA`, ns, numbers on the `timing` entry like `carryPulse`): `maxRise` 26 and `maxFall` 29 (clock to MAX; the SCEI-to-MAX 12/23 ns uses the same keys unless two more extras `maxEnableRise`/`maxEnableFall` are added). Otherwise `timing.TTL` is the main path: clock to QA-QD and segments `{"rise": 28, "fall": 28}`, `note` "clock to QA-QD tPLH typ 28 ns, tPHL not printed (sn74143 p.5)", `vcc` [4.75, 5.25].

Not modeled: the 15 mA / 7 mA constant-current behavior and the 1-5 V on-state / 7 V off-state output voltage limits (a 0 driver simply sinks), power dissipation, the "enables must not change while the clock is low" rule, input diode clamps and input resistor loads (SCEI 4 k, others 8 k, p.4), set-up/pulse-width violations (30 ns serial/parallel carry, 60 ns clear inactive, clock width 25 / 55 ns, clear width 25 ns, p.4).

## Bench plan for the model keys (for `counter_seg7`-style test, rows of the function table)

1. Power-on: shows 0, MAX H, RBO H. CLR L with RBI H: shows 0; with RBI L: all off, RBO low.
2. Rising CLK steps 0 to 9 and wraps; each glyph row and Q code above; MAX low only at 9 with SCEI low; SCEI high: MAX high at 9 and the clock does not count; PCEI high: no count, MAX still low at 9.
3. STRB high at count 5: Q and segments hold 5 through counts 6 and 7; STRB low: they jump to the counter value; MAX follows the counter, not the latch.
4. DP high lights dp (pin 8 low, open-drain), not while BI is high, RBI is low at code 0, or the `BI/RBO` pin is held low externally; the other segment pins read Z when off.
5. BI high: all pins Z and `BI/RBO` low; RBI low with the latch at 0: RBO low; at 1: RBO released; an external low on `BI/RBO` blanks every output.
6. Two digits (look-ahead of p.6): SCEI of the upper from the lower's MAX; lower 9 to 0 steps the upper once; the upper RBO to the lower RBI.
