## 1. Goal

- `CatalogPart.details` is `.battery(function:cells:cellNames:coinDiameterUM:)` for a connector with
  `battery` set; `symbol(for:)` gives `.battery`.
- The canvas draws `BatterySymbolView`: the IEC cell symbol (a long plate `+` and a short plate
  `−`), one pair per cell up to 4 and a dashed gap between the first and last pair beyond 4, the
  `+` pin on the left and the `−` pin on the right (left to right like the LED); a `contact` draws
  one plate with its one pin and the label `+`, `−` or `LINK`; a per-cell holder (`1+`, `1-` …)
  draws one cell per pin pair, stacked. Rotation and flip as for the LED.
- Details rows: Type (Holder / Retainer / Clip / Contact / Snap), Cell (`CR2032 class, 20 mm coin`,
  `AA`, `A / AA / CR2`), Cells, Pins.
- `ReferenceCategory.of` → `.battery` (prefix `BT`) for these parts.
- The drawer lists them under Connector → Battery → Coin / Cylindrical / 9 V.
- The Part Editor's connector form shows `battery`, `cell`, `coinDiameter`, `cells` for a battery
  device; `PartKindKeysTests` passes.
- KiCad and EasyEDA Pro schematic export write the battery symbol (KiCad's `Device:Battery` /
  `Device:Battery_Cell` shapes drawn as the app draws them), and the BOM lists battery parts.
- The Board Preview shows a holder as a housing of its footprint's body outline.

### Non-goals

- No battery voltage in play mode (plan-79-C); no new kind; no change to other connector symbols.

## 2. Design notes

- The coin "class" text (`CR2032 class`) comes from `coinDiameter`: 20 mm → `CR20xx`, 24.5 mm →
  `CR24xx`, 12 mm → `CR12xx`, 16 mm → `CR16xx`, 23 mm → `CR23xx` (the IEC CR size code is the
  diameter in mm); any other diameter shows `<d> mm coin` only.
- The symbol is a new `SymbolStyle.battery`; each file of the References' `.barrelJack` grep gets
  its `.battery` case (the compiler's exhaustive-switch errors list any missed).
