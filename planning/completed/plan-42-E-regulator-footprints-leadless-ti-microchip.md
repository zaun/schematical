and Microchip `add` rows — SON / DFN / WSON / VSON / X2SON, QFN / VQFN /

One line was missed by its packet and found by the follow-up `F-05`: `20-DSBGA` (TPS63024 YFF0020): `tps63024.pdf` p.27 carries the outline (the first packet looked for a land pattern); drawn as `DSBGA-20-YFF` (grid 5 x 4, pitch 0.4, ball 0.25 → pad 0.20).
3. **Judgement calls:** the mixed `4-XFBGA, DSBGA | 4-DSBGA` line (three land patterns YFP, YPF = YZR, YKE) is split by datasheet (`footprint-map.tsv` rows for `tlv705.pdf`, `lp5900.pdf`, `lp5907.pdf`); `DSBGA-5-YZU` serves TPS799L's YZY0005 (no land pattern in its PDF; ball stagger identical); Micrel legacy datasheets without a land pattern use Microchip's own land pattern of the identical outline (MIC5219 / MIC47050 / MIC5319 / MIC94325 TDFN / DFN22-6LD-PL-1); the "12-MLF (4x4)" DigiKey line is the 3x3 DFN33-12LD-PL-1 package (all four datasheets show 3x3); the MCP1665 VQFN-10 exposed pad is fused with pin 10 (explicit, two abutting pin-10 pads); `X2SON-4-DQN` centre land: the drawing's 0.48 square turned 45° overlaps the signal lands' boxes in the loader's overlap check, so it is a hexagon (below).
4. **Hand checks** (`fp-check-E.tsv`: 11 objects of 4 families, DFN / SON / MLF, X2SON, QFN, ball arrays): 10 ok, 1 fixed: `X2SON-4-DQN` (tlv713p.pdf p.37): the author's centre land was a 0.44 diamond (42 % of the drawn area); a 0.68 x 0.44 rectangle with all four corners cut 0.22 covers 88 % of the drawn 0.48 square turned 45° (0.679 diagonal), its box only touches the land boxes (allowed), and the signal land chamfer is 0.18 as drawn.

## Summary

Leadless packages are where pad mistakes hide (exposed pads, pull-back,
corner pins). The datasheets' own recommended land patterns are the source,
and one hand check per family per maker guards them.
