# G07 report

Devices written: 74_641, 74_756, 74_1245, 1G07, 1T34, 1T34_DUAL, 74_16244,
74_16646, 1G240, 74_29827, 74_162543 (11). Parts placed: 165 of 168.

## Per device

### 74_641 (buffer / transceiver, open-drain)
- PDFs: sn74ls641 (scanned; read as images), sn74als641a, sn74as641 (same file as sn74als641a).
- Families: LS, ALS, AS.
- The outputs are open-collector on **both** A and B ports. The model is `output: open-drain`.
- The LS datasheet calls the enable `G` (bar). It is named `OE` here, like the ALS/AS sheet, so that
  every pinout uses the same names.
- Timing: LS uses typical values; ALS and AS use max values (the sheet prints no typical).
- Part notes:
  - SN74LS641DW: the PDF table lists it as **Obsolete**; the input says Active. It is placed anyway.
  - These parts are not in their PDF's packaging table but are placed (same 20-pin pinout):
    SN74ALS641A-1DB (DB SSOP, a package the PDF never mentions), SN74ALS641ANS, SN74LS641-1N3,
    SN74LS641NS.

### 74_756 (buffer / tristate, inverting, open-drain)
- PDFs: sn74as756, sn74bct756. Families: AS, BCT.
- The census put this in the "buffer" bucket, while plan-20-A §3.1 puts '756 under gate `buf`. But the
  '756 has 1OE/2OE (both active low) and **inverts**: see the sn74bct756 function table, and the
  output-negation marks in the sn74as756 logic symbol. So it is written as an inverting open-drain
  `tristate` with 2 units.
- AS timing is max only.
- SN74AS756DWE4 is not in the PDF table; it is placed (a DW variant).

### 74_1245 (buffer / transceiver)
- PDFs: sn54als1245a (same file as sn74als1245a). Family: ALS.
- The FK pin numbers match the DIP numbers.
- SN54 and SN74 share VCC 4.5–5.5 V, so there is no partVcc.
- SN74ALS1245A-1DW is not in the PDF table (the table lists only N, FK and J); it is placed on the
  DW pinout from p.1. SN74ALS1245AN is Last Time Buy in the PDF.

### 1G07 (gate, buf, open-drain)
- PDFs: sn74auc1g07, sn74aup1g07, sn74lvc1g07, sn74lvc1g07-ep, sn74lvc1g07-q1.
- Families: AUC, AUP, LVC.
- Pinouts:
  - p5: DBV, DCK, DRL, DPW
  - p6: DRY, DSF (NC at pins 1 and 5)
  - dsbga: YZP, with A1 = DNU, in the 1G04 style
  - dsbga4: YZV
- Unlike the existing `1G00_SON` split, the SON variant is a second pinout here, as the brief allows.

### 1T34 (gate, buf) — LV only
- PDFs: sn74lv1t34, sn74lv1t34-q1. Family: LV.
- Follows the existing 1T00/1T04 style: single supply, reduced input thresholds, VI 0–5.5 V
  (= VCC max, so no inputMax).

### 1T34_DUAL (buffer / translator) — AUP1T34
- PDFs: sn74aup1t34, sn74aup1t34-q1. Family: AUP.
- **The census bucket "gate" is wrong.** SN74AUP1T34 is a dual-supply (VCCA/VCCB), 1-bit,
  unidirectional A→B translator with no enable. It is written as a translator in the tristate form
  (`a: [A]`, `y: [B]`, `oe: []`).
- Pinouts:
  - p5 (DCK): VCCA, A, GND, B, VCCB
  - p6 (DRY/DSF): adds NC at pin 5, VCCB at pin 6
- The id is a placeholder; rename it if the generator has a convention (it cannot share `1T34`'s
  device sense).
- Timing: max at VCCA 1.65 V → VCCB 3 V, 15 pF. This is the nearest row to a 1.8 → 3.3 V
  translation; the part's maximum is 3.6 V. VCC and VCCB are 0.9–3.6 V. The recommended operating
  conditions give no VI row, so there is no inputMax.

### 74_16244 (buffer / tristate, 4 units)
- PDFs: all 22 in the input. Families: AC, ACT, ABT (+ABTH), AHC, AHCT, ALB, ALVC (+ALVCH),
  AUC (+AUCH), AVC, LVC (+LVCH/LVCZ/EP/Q1), LVT (+LVTH/EP/SP), FCT.
- The pinout was checked in every PDF and is identical, including the WD CFP (5962-9668501VXA) and
  the Cypress SSOP.
- partVcc: SN74LVCZ uses 2.7–3.6 V.
- LVT has two main PDFs (sn74lvt16244b and sn74lvth16244a) with identical numbers.
- AVC timing is max at 2.5 V; ALVC and LVC timing is max at 3.3 V.
- The FCT parts are a problem. CY74FCT16244ETPVC/ETPVCT ("E" speed grade) and 74FCT16244TPVCG4 are
  not in cy74fct16244t's ordering or packaging tables, and every CY74FCT16244 row in that table is
  Obsolete. They are placed on FCT with the base-grade 6.5 ns.
- These parts are also not in their PDF's table but are placed (the non-R DGG reels):
  74AHCT16244DLRG4, SN74ABT16244ADGG, SN74ALB16244DGG, SN74ALVC16244ADGG, SN74LVCH16244ADGG.

### 74_16646 (buffer / registered, '646 form)
- PDFs: 74act16646, sn74abt16646, sn74alvch16646, sn74lvc16646a, sn74lvch16646a, sn74lvt16646,
  sn74lvth16646, sn74avc16646, cy74fct16646t.
- The pinout is identical in all of them.
- Unit-level `dir` + `oe` (OE active low, DIR high = A→B). Per direction: `store: register` on the
  CLKAB/CLKBA rising edge, `select` SAB/SBA (high = stored).
- Timing is clock → bus.
- LVT: sn74lvt16646 (3.8/3.9 typ) and sn74lvth16646 (2.8/2.8 typ) differ. The LVT entry uses
  sn74lvt16646, the base family's own sheet, even though 3 of the 4 LVT-family parts are LVTH.
- sn74avc16646 says "Input disabled" for its isolation/hold row (dynamic inputs). This is not
  modeled.
- Part notes:
  - CY74FCT16646ETPVC/ETPVCT: the PDF table says OBSOLETE (the input says Active). They are placed
    with the base-grade 9.0 ns.
  - SN74AVC16646DGG and SN74LVC16646ADGG are not in their PDF tables; they are placed.

### 1G240 (buffer / tristate, inverting)
- PDFs: sn74auc1g240, sn74aup1g240, sn74lvc1g240. Families: AUC, AUP, LVC.
- Pinouts:
  - p5: DBV, DCK, DPW (1 OE, 2 A, 3 GND, 4 Y, 5 VCC)
  - p6: DRY, DSF
  - dsbga: YZP (A1 OE, B1 A, C1 GND, C2 Y, A2 VCC)
- **Odd:** the YZP top-view figure in sn74lvc1g240 (p.3) draws pin 4 (Y) top-right and pin 5 (VCC)
  bottom-right. That disagrees with sn74aup1g240 and sn74auc1g240, which put VCC at A2 (next to OE)
  and Y at C2 (next to GND). The balls follow the AUP/AUC sheets (the standard 1G YZP map).
- SN74LVC1G240DBVRE4 is not in the PDF table; it is placed (DBV).

### 74_29827 (buffer / tristate)
- PDFs: sn74als29827, sn74bct29827b. Families: ALS, BCT.
- One 10-bit unit whose `oe` is [OE1 low, OE2 low]: the enable is a NOR gate, so the outputs drive
  only while both are low.
- ALS timing is max at 50 pF.

### 74_162543 (buffer / registered, latched '543 form)
- PDF: cy74fct162543t. Family: FCT.
- Per direction: `store: latch` with `le` LEAB/LEBA (active low), `ce` CEAB/CEBA (active low) and
  `oe` OEAB/OEBA (active low).
- Timing: A→B in transparent mode, max, base grade.
- The parts span several speed grades:
  - CY74FCT162543CTPAC is C grade, on the CY-PA TSSOP. It is not in the PDF's packaging table (only
    74FCT162543CTPACT is).
  - CY74FCT162543ETPVC is E grade; the table lists it as OBSOLETE.
  - 74FCT162543TPVCG4 is Active.
  - All three are placed.

### 74_162543 (cy74fct162543t p.2; A→B shown, B→A uses CEBA/LEBA/OEBA the same way)

| CEAB | LEAB | OEAB | A→B latch | B outputs |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| H | X | X | Storing | High Z |
| X | H | X | Storing | X (per table) |
| X | X | H | X | High Z |
| L | L | L | Transparent | Current A inputs |
| L | H | L | Storing | Previous A inputs |

Text (p.1): "With CEAB LOW, a LOW signal on LEAB makes the A-to-B latches transparent; a subsequent
LOW-to-HIGH transition of LEAB puts the A latches in the storage mode… With CEAB and OEAB both LOW,
the 3-state B output buffers are active and reflect the data present at the output of the A
latches."
