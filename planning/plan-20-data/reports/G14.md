### Things to know

- **'17 is split in two.** SN7417 is a TTL hex buffer with open-collector 15 V
  outputs. AC17, ACT17 and AUC17 are push-pull Schmitt-trigger buffers. The
  `output` key covers the whole device, so these are two devices: `74_17`
  (push-pull) and `74_17_OD` (TTL).
- **'367 and '368 are split by pin names, as the brief asks.** The pins sit in
  the same places, but the names differ:
  - SN74HC/AHC/AHCT/HCS/LV use `1OE`, `2OE`, `1A1..1A4`, `2A1..2A2`, `1Y1..`.
  - SN74LS367A/368A (one scanned data sheet) name the enables `1G`, `2G`. This
    gives `_LS`.
  - CD74HC/HCT367/368 (one data sheet) use `OE1`, `OE2`, `1A..6A`, `1Y..6Y`.
    This gives `_CD`.

  Library precedent (counter 74_590, shift 74_597) merges such cases into one
  device with a note. If you prefer that, the three specs can be merged under
  the SN names. In every case the units are 4 bits on enable 1 and 2 bits on
  enable 2, both enables active low.
- **74_639 has open-collector outputs on one side only.** The A-bus outputs
  are open-collector and the B-bus outputs are 3-state (sn74als639a p.1). The
  spec says `"output": "push-pull"`, so the A side's open collector is not
  modeled.
- **1G126 must be merged with G15's 1G126.** The input function `1P1G126` is
  the SN74LVC1G126-Q1 die sold as 1P1G126QDBVRQ1 and 1P1G126QDRYRQ1. Its p5
  pinout (OE, A, GND, Y, VCC) is the standard 1G126 one. DRY is a 6-pin SON
  with NC on pin 5, given as pinout `son6`. If G15 splits SON parts into a
  `1G126_SON` device, move the DRY part there.
- **TXU0202 OE domain.** The datasheet lets OE take VCCA or VCCB logic levels
  (txu0202 p.3). It is placed in the VCCA domain.
- **4041UB.** Each input has a true output (`buf`) and a complement output
  (`not`), so there are 8 gates and `units: 4`. The data sheet is scanned; the
  pinout comes from the p.1 image.
- **Translator timing.** No translator here prints a typical delay, so all
  are max values:
  - LVC, LXC and TXU use the VCCA 3.3 V → VCCB 5 V row.
  - The 3.6 V families (AVC, AXC) use VCCA = VCCB = 3.3 V, the row nearest
    3.3 ↔ 5 V.
  - No translator gets `inputMax`/`inputMaxB`: the VI max equals the VCC max
    in every one.
- **The -Q1 data sheets of AVC1T45 and AVC4T774 give VCC 1.08-3.6 V; the
  main data sheets give 1.2 or 1.1-3.6 V.** Per the brief, the main data
  sheet's range is used.
- **The scanned LS data sheet has SN54LS rows at 4.5-5.5 V.** `partVcc` has
  prefixes `SN54` and `SNJ54`.
- **The TTL '17 has an SNJ5417J row at 4.5-5.5 V.** `partVcc` prefix `SNJ54`.
- **CD74HC/HCT367/368 timing.** The only typical is at 5 V and 15 pF (8, 9,
  9 and 11 ns). At 4.5 V and 50 pF only the max is printed (21-30 ns). The
  spec uses the typical.
- **2G17 LVC timing** is the max at 3.3 V (5.4 ns); no typical is printed.
  **ALS639A** has max only (12 ns).
