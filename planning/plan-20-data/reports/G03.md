# G03 report — plan-20 buffer specs

196 input rows: 188 placed in 7 devices, 8 not placed.

## Devices

### 74_240 (`tristate`, invert, 2 units × 4 bits, 1OE / 2OE active low) — 167 parts
- PDFs: cd74ac240, cd74act240, cd74hc240, cd74hct240, cy54fct240t, cy74fct240t, sn54abt240, sn54ahc240, sn54ahct240, sn54als240a, sn54as240a, sn54bct240, sn54f240, sn54hc240, sn54hct240, sn54ls240, sn54lvth240, sn54s240, sn74abt240a, sn74ac240, sn74ac240-q1, sn74act240, sn74act240-q1, sn74ahc240, sn74ahc240-q1, sn74ahct240, sn74ahct240-q1, sn74als240a, sn74as240a, sn74auc240, sn74bct240, sn74f240, sn74hc240, sn74hcs240, sn74hcs240-q1, sn74hct240, sn74ls240, sn74lv240a, sn74lvc240a, sn74lvc240a-q1, sn74lvcz240a, sn74lvt240a, sn74lvth240, sn74s240. Many of these files are the same document (for example sn74ls240 = sn54ls240 = sn74s240 = sn54s240, and sn74als240a = sn74as240a).
- Families: HC, HCT, HCS, LS, S, F, ALS, AS, AC, ACT, AHC, AHCT, ABT, BCT, LVT (LVT and LVTH), LV, LVC (LVC and LVCZ), AUC, FCT.
- Every package uses one 20-pin pinout (`p20`), including FK/LCCC, CY L-LCC, RKS/RGY VQFN (the pad is not a pin) and CY QSOP. I checked this against each sheet's pin drawing or table.
- Pin names: I folded the variant names into the TI ones, as plan-10 did for '373. LS/S sheets print 1G/2G for the enables. CD74HC/HCT/AC/ACT number the bits from 0 (1A0..1A3, 2Y0..2Y3). CY FCT prints OEA/OEB, DA0-3/DB0-3 and OA0-3/OB0-3. The pin functions are the same at every position; the mapping is in the device note.
- Odd:
  - FCT: one timing entry for three speed grades. I used the base CY74FCT240T at 8 ns max; the AT grade is 4.8 ns and the CT grade 4.3 ns (cy74fct240t p.7).
  - The cy74fct240t function table only prints the rows where both enables match. The logic diagram (p.3) shows OEA gating DA→OA and OEB gating DB→OB, so I modeled it as the standard '240.
  - ALS, AS, LVC and FCT print no typical delay, so I used the max (each note says so).
- `partVcc` overrides:
  - SNJ54LS/SNJ54S → 4.5-5.5 V (the LS/S entries are 4.75-5.25 V).
  - CY54 and 5962-92213 → 4.5-5.5 V (FCT entry is 4.75-5.25 V).
  - CD74AC240 → 1.5-5.5 V (AC entry is 2-6 V).
  - SN74LVCZ → 2.7-3.6 V, inputs to 5.5 V (LVC entry is 1.65-3.6 V).
- Status flags:
  - SN74HC240DBR: input says Active, but the sn74hc240 packaging table lists it as **NRND**.
  - These names are in the input but **not in their PDF's packaging table**. I placed them anyway (same package codes, same pinout):
    - CY74FCT240ATQC, CY74FCT240CTQC (code CY-QC = QSOP; CTQC also has no pin count)
    - SN74ABT240ADB, SN74ABT240ADBLE (code DBL, pins "?" — 20-pin SSOP), SN74ABT240ANS, SN74ABT240APWLE
    - SN74AC240NS, SN74ACT240PWLE, SN74AHC240NS, SN74AS240ANS, SN74BCT240NS
    - SN74HC240AN, SN74HC240ANSR, SN74HC240APWR, SN74HCT240ANSR
    - SN74LS240DB, SN74LVC240ANS, SN74LVT240ADB, SN74LVTH240DB, SN74LVTH240NS, SN74S240N3
  - 74ALVCH16825DLR (in 74_16825) is the same case.

### 4050B (`gate`, 6 × `buf`) — 5 parts
- PDF: cd4050b. Family: C.
- The pinout keeps the sheet's names (inputs A-F, outputs G-L, NC on 13 and 16), as the existing `4049UB` does. The pin table's "inverting output" wording was copied from the CD4049UB; the 4050B is non-inverting.
- I set `inputMax` to 18 V: the sheet says the input high level may exceed VCC and rates input current at 18 V. The existing 4049UB entry has no inputMax, so drop it if that should stay consistent.
- CD4050BDWR: input says Active, but cd4050b p.20 lists it as **NRND**.

### 74_4050 (`gate`, 6 × `buf`) — 4 parts

- `inputMax` is 15 V, from the DC input voltage 0-15 V recommended range (p.3).

### 74_8T541 (`tristate`, 8 bits, OE1 and OE2 both active low) — 6 parts
- PDFs: sn74lv8t541, sn74lv8t541-q1. Family: LV, with its own entry: 1.65-5.5 V, inputs to 5.5 V.
- This is a **single-supply** LVxT part, so it gets no `domains` and the `tristate` model, not `translator` (plan-20-A §3.1 lists 8T541 under translator).
- The switching table prints only min and max, so timing is the 3.3 V, 50 pF max.

### TXV0108 (`translator`, transceiver form: DIR high = A→B, OE active low) — 1 part
- PDF: txv0108. Family: TXV.
- Domains: VCCA = DIR, OE, A1-A8; VCCB = B1-B8.
- **Two VCCB pins** (23 and 24) share the name `VCCB`. If the domain `power` has to resolve to exactly one pin, the loader needs to accept this, or one pin needs another name.
- Both ports run 1.14-3.6 V, so no 3.3↔5 V row exists. I used the VCCA 3.3 V / VCCB 3.3 V row: tpd 2.3 ns max, no typical printed.
- The 10-ohm damping resistors are not modeled.

## Does not fit
- **SN54SC245MPWTSEP** (sn54sc245-sep, function '245): its family is "SC" (radiation-tolerant, 1.2-5.5 V CMOS), which is not one of the allowed family codes. Otherwise it is a standard 74_245: pins DIR 1, A1-A8 2-9, GND 10, B8-B1 11-18, OE 19 (active low), VCC 20. DIR H = A→B. VCC 1.2-5.5 V, VI to 5.5 V (p.5). Typical tpd 5.8 ns at 5 V and 7.6 ns at 3.3 V, 50 pF (p.8). If the family question is settled, it fits the 74_245 device.
- **SN74ABT16470DGGR, SN74ABT16470DL** (sn74abt16470, 16-bit registered transceiver): CLKENAB high also forces the B outputs to Z. That is function table row 1 (p.2), and p.7 lists CLKEN→A/B tPZH/tPZL/tPHZ/tPLZ. The `registered` model has one `oe` control per direction, so the output enable would have to be OEAB-low AND CLKENAB-low. The rest fits:
  - Two units of 8 bits. Per direction: `register`, `clk` nCLKAB rising, `ce` nCLKENAB low, `oe` nOEAB low; BA the same with nCLKBA, nCLKENBA, nOEBA. No select.
  - ABT typical CLK→A/B: 3.1 / 3.2 ns at 5 V (p.7). VCC 4.5-5.5 V (p.5).
  - Pinout (p.1, DGG/DL):
    - 1 1OEAB, 2 1CLKAB, 3 1CLKENAB, 4 GND, 5 1A1, 6 1A2, 7 VCC, 8 1A3, 9 1A4, 10 1A5, 11 GND, 12 1A6, 13 1A7, 14 1A8
    - 15 2A1, 16 2A2, 17 2A3, 18 GND, 19 2A4, 20 2A5, 21 2A6, 22 VCC, 23 2A7, 24 2A8, 25 GND, 26 2CLKENAB, 27 2CLKAB, 28 2OEAB
    - 29 2OEBA, 30 2CLKBA, 31 2CLKENBA, 32 GND, 33 2B8, 34 2B7, 35 VCC, 36 2B6, 37 2B5, 38 2B4, 39 GND, 40 2B3, 41 2B2, 42 2B1
    - 43 1B8, 44 1B7, 45 1B6, 46 GND, 47 1B5, 48 1B4, 49 1B3, 50 VCC, 51 1B2, 52 1B1, 53 GND, 54 1CLKENBA, 55 1CLKBA, 56 1OEBA
  - Function table (A→B; B→A is the same with CLKENBA, CLKBA, OEBA):

    | CLKENAB | CLKAB | OEAB | A | B |
    |---|---|---|---|---|
    | H | X | X | X | Z |
    | X | X | H | X | Z |
    | L | L | L | X | B0 |
    | L | ↑ | L | L | L |
    | L | ↑ | L | H | H |
