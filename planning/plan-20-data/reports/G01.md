    SN74 names (plan-19 precedent: CY74FCT parts join the SN74 device). The
    mapping is in the device note.

    CY74FCT244T prints max only, per speed grade (T 6.5, AT 4.6, CT 4.1,
    DT 3.6 ns). The entry uses the CD74FCT244 typical; the CY grades are in
    the note.
  - **partVcc** (names whose ROC range differs from the family entry):
    SN54S → [4.5, 5.5]; CD74AC (family AC only; CD74ACT is 4.5-5.5 like the
    entry) → [1.5, 5.5]; SN74AC244Q…Q1 → [1.5, 6]; CY54 and 5962-92203…
    (CY54FCT244T SMDs) → [4.5, 5.5]; SN74LVCZ → [2.7, 3.6], inputMax 5.5.
    Every other -Q1/-EP/SP/SN54 PDF has the same range as its entry.
  - SMD family corrections from the packaging tables: 5962-9755901QRA is
    SNJ54AS244AJ → **AS** (not ALS); 5962-8683901SA / 5962-86839012A are
    SNJ54ALS244CW / CFK → ALS; 5962-8776001SSA = SNV54ACT244W → ACT;
    5962-9062501* = SNJ54BCT244 → BCT; 5962-9214701* (sn54abt244) → ABT;
    5962-9220301M2A = CY54FCT244TLMB, 5962-9220303M2A (CY54FCT244CT) → FCT;
    5962-9678201QSA = SNJ54AHC244W → AHC; 5962-9758601* = SNJ54F244 → F;
    JM38510/33203BRA (sn54f244) → F; JM38510/65705BSA (sn54hc244-sp) → HC;
    JM38510/65755B2A (sn54hct244-sp) → HCT; V62/03667-01YE
    (sn74lvth244a-ep, marking LH244AEP) → LVT.
