## Function tables of registered devices

`74_16952` (sn74abt16952 p.2; same in sn74alvch16952 p.1, sn74lvt16952 p.2,
sn74lvth16952 p.2) and `74_16952_CE` (sn74lvch16952a p.2, cy74fct16952t p.2,
with CEAB in place of CLKENAB). A→B is shown; B→A uses CLKENBA/CEBA, CLKBA
and OEBA. Each unit (1x, 2x) is independent.

| CLKENAB | CLKAB | OEAB | A | B |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| H | X | L | X | B0 (stored, unchanged) |
| X | L | L | X | B0 |
| L | ↑ | L | L | L |
| L | ↑ | L | H | H |
| X | X | H | X | Z |

Model: `ab` = {store register, clk CLKAB rising, ce CLKENAB low, oe OEAB low};
`ba` = {store register, clk CLKBA rising, ce CLKENBA low, oe OEBA low}. No
real-time path.
