### Verified properties

- **A trace never shorts a channel.** `tests/support/trace_core.c`
  (`stimulus_start`) drives input pins strongly and inout pins weakly, so a
  transistor's collector and emitter (both inout) never fight strongly through
  a joined channel and the golden trace of a transistor part ends without
  `smoke:`.
- **A footprint choice may have more pads than the part has pins**
  (docs/parts-format.md § The `"footprint"` part key), so a generic's
  three-pin pinout can offer a four-pad SOT-223.
- **`footprint_check` fails on a footprint no part uses** (same section), so
  the footprints of C–E are merged in this letter, with the parts.

## 3. Design

### 3.1 Devices

One device per (drawer, function, pinout):

- `drawer`: `["Discrete", "Transistors", "BJT"]` or `["Discrete",
  "Transistors", "MOSFET"]`; the generics `["Discrete", "Transistors"]`
  (the three-level drawer: docs/parts-format.md § Devices).
- `function` (the drawer leaf): BJT `NPN`, `PNP`, `NPN Darlington`, `PNP
  Darlington`, `NPN pre-biased`, `PNP pre-biased`, `Array` (any array, pre-biased
  or not); MOSFET `N-channel`, `N-channel logic-level`, `P-channel`, `P-channel
  logic-level`, `Array`; generics `Generic`. A MOSFET is logic-level when
  DigiKey's first gate drive of Rds(on) (`drive_v`) is 4.5 V or less;
  arrays give no drive voltage and are never split.
- `id`: `<bjt|mosfet>-<function slug>-<pinout key>` (`bjt-npn-p3-ebc`,
  `mosfet-n-channel-logic-level-p3-gds`, `bjt-array-p6-…`); the pinout key is
  `p<pins>-<pin names, lower case, joined>` (a ball pinout `p4-a1g-b1s-…`).
- `description`: `NPN bipolar transistor`, `NPN Darlington transistor`,
  `Pre-biased NPN transistor`, `N-channel MOSFET`, `N-channel logic-level MOSFET`,
  `Dual NPN / PNP transistor array`, …
- `pinouts`: one, from `specs.tsv`'s `pinout` (numbered, or ball): the control
  pin (`B`, `G`, `B1`, …) `input`; collector / drain / emitter / source `inout`; an
  `NC` pin `nc`; same-named pins (a tab) `inout`.
- `elements`: from `specs.tsv`'s `elements` (`{"type": "npn", "base": "B",
  "collector": "C", "emitter": "E"}`; `"darlington": true` for a Darlington).

### 3.2 Parts

`{"name": <mpn>, "manufacturer": <maker>, "package": <specs.tsv label>,
"pinout": <pinout key>, "footprint": <footprint-map.tsv id>, "datasheet": <id>}`
plus the facts of DigiKey's columns: BJT `vceo` = `vmax_v`, `ic` = `imax_a`, `pd`
= `power_w`, `hfe` = `hfe_min`, a pre-biased part's `r1`, `r2` = `r1_ohm`,
`r2_ohm`; MOSFET `vds` = `vmax_v`, `id` = `imax_a`, `pd` = `power_w`, `rdson` =
`rdson_ohm`, `vgsth` = `vgsth_v`, `vgs` = `vgs_v`, `logicLevel` true for a
logic-level one (a value that is absent or 0 is left out). Datasheet ids:
`<maker-slug>-<PDF stem, lowercased, non-alphanumerics → ->`, one
`datasheets.json` entry per PDF with its URL from
`datasheets/transistor/datasheets.tsv`.

### 3.3 The generics

Devices `generic-npn`, `generic-pnp`, `generic-nmos`, `generic-pmos`
(function `Generic`, drawer `["Discrete", "Transistors"]`, one element each,
pinout `p3`: pins `1` B / G, `2` C / D, `3` E / S, the generic's own
numbering on every footprint choice). Parts `GENERIC-NPN`, `GENERIC-PNP`,
`GENERIC-NMOS`, `GENERIC-PMOS`: manufacturer `Generic`, no datasheet, no
ratings, package `TO-92` (footprint the `TO-92` footprint of the most-stocked
`add` part whose `label` is `TO-92`), and `footprintChoices`: `TO-92`, `SOT-23`,
`SOT-223`, `TO-220`, `DPAK`, each the footprint of the most-stocked `add` part
whose `label` is that package (`footprint-map.tsv`), `TO-92` first (the
default). Every choice has at least the three pads.

### 3.4 Where the risk is

A generator bug that puts a part on the wrong footprint or pinout, which
`library_check` (pin counts against pads) and the golden review catch; a name
clash across makers (`BC847B` from two makers) that `load_parts` rejects as a
duplicate part name.

## Decisions

- **One device per (leaf, pinout)** — author: the drawer leaf is the device's
  `function`, so the type and the logic-level split must be device fields;
  the pinout is the device's.
- **The generics' own pin numbers** — author: a generic has no datasheet; the
  same three pins on every footprint choice keep one pinout for them all.
- **Part names are DigiKey's part numbers** — author: the resistor and
  capacitor plans' practice; B's ordering table is what makes them orderable.
- **Choices by stock** — author: the footprint a generic offers for a package
  is the one of the most-stocked real part of that package.
- Owner decisions: plan-40-A § Decisions.
