## 3. Design

### 3.1 – 3.2 The scheme and the status

As plan-54-B § 3.1 and § 3.2. For a Samtec print the scheme is built from the
ordering key's fields: each `XX` field becomes the alternation of its listed
options and the position field the set of counts the print lists (`HSEC8-1[0-9]{2}-01-[LFS]-DV-A-K(-TR)?`). A row
whose key field allows an option the print does not list for that series is
`skip` (`not in the datasheet's ordering table`).

### 3.3 Pin designators and pinouts

`pinout` is one of:

- `seq` — pins `1` … `N`, named `1` … `N` (the datasheet numbers the contacts
  1 … N);
- `AB` — `A1` … `A<N/2>` then `B1` … `B<N/2>` named by designator (N even; the
  datasheet labels the two sides A and B, as a PCI Express edge does);
- an explicit list `1=NAME,2=NAME,…` — odd position counts, row-letter schemes
  other than A / B, or signal names the datasheet's pin table prints (a PCI
  Express or M.2 pin table; a part the datasheet's table does not name by
  signal uses `seq` or `AB`).

Every pin is a passive `inout` terminal (`nc` for a contact the datasheet marks
not connected). Mounting pegs, board-lock holes and shell-less hold-downs are
footprint pads with no pin; a metal shell or hold-down tab that the datasheet
connects to ground is a `SHIELD` pin (as USB). A pinout with more than 40 pins
gets `"side": "in"` on its first half (M and N add the key).

### 3.4 Facts

`card`: `PCI Express`, `M.2`, `MicroTCA`, `MXM 3.0`, or `Dual edge` / `Single edge`
for DigiKey's "Non Specified" (the words of DigiKey's Card Type with `™` and
"Non Specified -" dropped). `pitch` (mm) is the contact pitch of the drawing,
checked against DigiKey's Pitch (a difference is `skip`, reason `fact not in
datasheet`, unless the drawing's pitch is the rounding of DigiKey's: 0.635
against 0.64). Rows is 1 or 2 from the drawing.

**Risk**: schemes for Samtec keys with many option fields (a too-wide regex
passes a part the print does not list) and the 49 odd-count pinouts; the
`--sample` hand check of Phase 1 and the pin-count check of Phase 3 bound both.
