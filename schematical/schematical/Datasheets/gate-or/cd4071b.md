# CD4071B, CD4072B, CD4075B CMOS OR gates

## Description

High-voltage types (20-volt rating): CD4071B quad 2-input OR gate,
CD4072B dual 4-input OR gate, CD4075B triple 3-input OR gate.
CD4071B, CD4072B, and CD4075B OR gates provide the system designer with
direct implementation of the positive-logic OR function and supplement
the existing family of CMOS gates. [[1::2]]

Medium-speed operation: t~PLH~, t~PHL~ = 60 ns (typ.) at V~DD~ = 10 V.
100% tested for quiescent current at 20 V. Maximum input current of
1 µA at 18 V over full package-temperature range; 100 nA at 18 V and
25°C. Standardized, symmetrical output characteristics. Noise margin
(over full package temperature range): 1 V at V~DD~ = 5 V, 2 V at
V~DD~ = 10 V, 2.5 V at V~DD~ = 15 V. 5-V, 10-V, and 15-V parametric
ratings. Meets all requirements of JEDEC Tentative Standard No. 13B,
"Standard Specifications for Description of 'B' Series CMOS Devices". [[1::4]]

The CD4071B, CD4072B, and CD4075B types are supplied in 14-lead
hermetic dual-in-line ceramic packages (F3A suffix), 14-lead
dual-in-line plastic packages (E suffix), 14-lead small-outline
packages (M, MT, M96, and NSR suffixes), and 14-lead thin shrink
small-outline packages (PW and PWR suffixes). [[1::3]]

## Conversion notes

- Source pages 1–4 converted (the Harris data sheet). Dropped: the
  older 2009 package option addendum and package materials pages
  (5–10), the PW mechanical page (13), blank pages, and the current
  package option addendum, tape and reel, tube and mechanical pages
  (16–34), replaced by the generated Package and Ordering sections.
  Ordering packing, temperature and marking columns are from the
  current (2026) package option addendum (p.16).
- Pages 1–4 are scans; every value was read from the page images
  (130 dpi, the static electrical characteristics table and the
  terminal assignments at 240 to 300 dpi) and checked there.
- Dropped: the functional diagrams and terminal-assignment drawings
  (pinouts are generated; the gate equations are in the function
  table), the schematic diagrams (Figs. 3, 7, 11), the chip dimensions
  and pad layouts (p.4), and the typical-characteristic curves
  (Figs. 1, 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 14: voltage transfer, propagation delay
  and transition time vs load capacitance, output sink and source
  current, dynamic power dissipation vs frequency), which the scan
  shows too coarsely to digitize to 2 %.
- The static electrical characteristics table prints one unit for
  I~OL~ and I~OH~ together, one for V~OL~ and V~OH~ and one for V~IL~
  and V~IH~; it is written on every row.
- The package option addendum also lists 7706002CA, JM38510/17101BCA,
  JM38510/17103BCA, M38510/17101BCA and M38510/17103BCA, which the
  catalog doesn't carry.
- From images: the names t~PLH~, t~PHL~ (features, p.1), I~DD~, I~OL~,
  I~OH~, V~OL~, V~OH~, V~IL~, V~IH~, I~IN~ (static electrical
  characteristics, p.1), P~D~, R~L~, t~THL~, t~TLH~, C~IN~ (maximum
  ratings and dynamic characteristics, p.2) and DVM (Fig. 17, p.4); the
  scans have no usable text layer for them.
- From images: every value of the features, the recommended operating
  conditions and the static electrical characteristics (p.1; the page
  has a text layer only for the description), among them 7.5, 0.64,
  0.61, 0.42, 1.1, 1.3, 2.6, 4.2, 2.8, 2.4, 3.4, 6.8, 4.6, 3.2, 9.5,
  13.5, 4.95, 9.95, 14.95, which no other page prints; 500 and 7.5
  (maximum ratings and dynamic characteristics, p.2). The I~OL~ +25°C
  Min at 15 V is printed "3 4" and read as 3.4.
- Derived: ±0.00001 (I~IN~ typical, printed ±10^-5^ µA, p.1).
- Source issues:
  - Source conflict: the functional diagrams (p.1) and the terminal
    assignments (p.4) letter the inputs of a gate differently. CD4071B:
    the functional diagram has B on pin 1 and A on pin 2 (likewise D/C
    on 5/6, F/E on 8/9, H/G on 12/13), the terminal assignments have A
    on pin 1 and B on pin 2 (C/D, E/F, G/H). CD4075B: the functional
    diagram has C on pin 1 and A on pin 8, F on pin 3 and D on pin 5,
    the terminal assignments have A on pin 1 and C on pin 8, D on pin 3
    and F on pin 5. The gate outputs and functions are the same in
    both; the function table and the generated pinouts follow the
    terminal assignments.
