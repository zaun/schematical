# SN54F32, SN74F32 quadruple 2-input positive-OR gates

## Description

These devices contain four independent 2-input OR gates. They perform
the Boolean functions Y = A + B or Y = !{!{A} • !{B}} in positive logic. [[1::2]]

The SN54F32 is characterized for operation over the full military
temperature range of –55°C to 125°C. The SN74F32 is characterized for
operation from 0°C to 70°C. [[1::3]]
