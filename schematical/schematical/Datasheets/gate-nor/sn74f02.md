# SN54F02, SN74F02 quadruple 2-input positive-NOR gates

## Description

These devices contain four independent 2-input NOR gates. They perform
the Boolean functions Y = !{A + B} or Y = !{A} • !{B} in positive logic.

The SN54F02 is characterized for operation over the full military
temperature range of –55°C to 125°C. The SN74F02 is characterized for
operation from 0°C to 70°C.
