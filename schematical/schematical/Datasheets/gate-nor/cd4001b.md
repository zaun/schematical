# CD4001B, CD4002B, CD4025B CMOS NOR gates

## Description

High-voltage types (20-volt rating): quad 2 input CD4001B, dual 4
input CD4002B, triple 3 input CD4025B.
CD4001B, CD4002B, and CD4025B NOR gates provide the system designer
with direct implementation of the NOR function and supplement the
existing family of CMOS gates. All inputs and outputs are buffered. [[1::2]]

Propagation delay time = 60 ns (typ.) at C~L~ = 50 pF, V~DD~ = 10 V.
Standardized symmetrical output characteristics. 100% tested for
maximum quiescent current at 20 V. 5-V, 10-V, and 15-V parametric
ratings. Maximum input current of 1 µA at 18 V over full
package-temperature range; 100 nA at 18 V and 25°C. Noise margin (over
full package temperature range): 1 V at V~DD~ = 5 V, 2 V at
V~DD~ = 10 V, 2.5 V at V~DD~ = 15 V. Meets all requirements of JEDEC
Tentative Standard No. 13B, "Standard Specifications for Description
of "B" Series CMOS Devices". [[1::4]]

The CD4001B, CD4002B, and CD4025B types are supplied in 14-lead
hermetic dual-in-line ceramic packages (F3A suffix), 14-lead
dual-in-line plastic packages (E suffix), 14-lead small-outline
packages (M, MT, M96, and NSR suffixes), and 14-lead thin shrink
small-outline packages (PW and PWR suffixes). [[1::3]]

## Conversion notes

- Source pages 1–4 converted (the Harris data sheet). Dropped: the
  package option addendum, tape and reel, tube and mechanical pages and
  the notice (5–23), replaced by the generated Package and Ordering
  sections. Ordering packing, temperature and marking columns are from
  the package option addendum (p.5).
- Pages 1–4 are scans; every value was read from the page images
  (110 dpi, the static electrical characteristics table, the functional
  diagrams and the terminal assignments at 220 to 300 dpi) and checked
  there.
- Dropped: the functional diagrams and terminal-assignment drawings
  (pinouts are generated; the gate equations are in the function
  table), the schematic diagrams (Figs. 5 to 7), the chip dimensions
  and pad layouts (p.4), and the typical-characteristic curves
  (Figs. 1 to 4 and 8 to 11: voltage transfer, power dissipation vs
  frequency, output low and high current, transition time and
  propagation delay time vs load capacitance), which the scan shows too
  coarsely to digitize to 2 %. The source has no Fig. 12.
- The static electrical characteristics table prints one unit for
  I~OL~ and I~OH~ together, one for V~OL~ and V~OH~ and one for V~IL~
  and V~IH~; it is written on every row.
- The package option addendum also lists 7704403CA, JM38510/05252BCA,
