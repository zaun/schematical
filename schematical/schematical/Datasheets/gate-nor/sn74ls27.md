# SN5427, SN54LS27, SN7427, SN74LS27 triple 3-input positive-NOR gates

## Description

These devices contain three independent 3-input NOR gates. [[1::2]]

The SN5427 and SN54LS27 are characterized for operation over the full
military temperature range of –55°C to 125°C. The SN7427 and SN74LS27
are characterized for operation from 0°C to 70°C. [[1::3]]

## Conversion notes

- Source pages 1–4 converted; the package option addendum (pp.5–8),
  tape and reel and mechanical pages (9–22) dropped, replaced by the
  generated Package and Ordering sections. Ordering packing, temperature
  and marking columns are from the package option addendum.
- Pages 1–4 are scans with no text layer; every value and sentence on
  them was read from the page images (100 and 170 dpi) and checked
  there.
- Dropped: feature list, the vendor pinout drawings, the logic symbol
  and the gate schematics (only mentioned in Functional description,
  without resistor values).
- The data sheet also covers the SN5427 and SN7427, which the catalog
  does not carry and the package option addendum does not list; their
  tables (p.3) are kept.
- The absolute maximum ratings of the source print a symbol for the
  supply voltage only; no other symbol is added. The electrical
  characteristics tables of the source give symbols only, so they have
  no Parameter column.
- "See Note 2" in the I~CCL~ rows is the source's NOTE 2, printed under
  each electrical characteristics table; the † ‡ § marks and NOTE 1 to
  NOTE 3 of the source are numbered per table here.
- From images: 7, 5.5, -55, 125, 0, 70, -65, 150 (absolute maximum
  ratings, p.2); 4.5, 5, 5.25, 4.75, 2, 0.8, -0.8, 16, -12, -1.5, 2.4,
  3.4, 0.2, 0.4, 1, 40, -1.6, -20, -18, 10, 26, 400, 15, 11 (p.3); 0.7,
  -0.4, 4, 8, -18, 2.5, 2.7, 0.25, 0.35, 0.5, 0.1, 20, -100, 3.4, 6.8
  (p.4); overbars of Y = !{A + B + C} and Y = !{A} · !{B} · !{C}
  (positive logic, p.1).
- Source issues: none found.
