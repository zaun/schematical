# SN54HC4002, SN74HC4002 dual 4-input positive-NOR gates

## Description

These devices contain two independent 4-input positive NOR gates. They
perform the Boolean functions Y = !{A + B + C + D} or
Y = !{A}·!{B}·!{C}·!{D} in positive logic. [[1::1]]

The SN54HC4002 is characterized for operation over the full military
temperature range of –55°C to 125°C. The SN74HC4002 is characterized
for operation from –40°C to 85°C. [[1::1]]

Package Options Include Plastic "Small Outline" Packages, Ceramic Chip
Carriers, and Standard Plastic and Ceramic 300-mil DIPs. [[1::1]]

## Conversion notes

- Source pages 1–3 converted; the package option addendum, tube and
  mechanical pages (4–11) dropped, replaced by the generated Package
  and Ordering sections. Ordering packing, temperature, marking and
  status columns are from the package option addendum (p.4).
- Pages 1–3 are scans with no text layer; every value on them was read
  from the page images (110 to 200 dpi) and checked there.
- Dropped: the "Dependable Texas Instruments Quality and Reliability"
  feature line, the logic symbol with its note on ANSI/IEEE Std 91-1984
  and IEC Publication 617-12, and the logic diagram (two 4-input NOR
  gates, 1A to 1D to 1Y and 2A to 2D to 2Y).
- The source prints no pin table; the pin descriptions are the
  converter's, except NC, which the FK drawing explains.
- The electrical and switching tables print symbols only, so they have
  no Parameter column. In the absolute maximum ratings the lead
  temperature and storage temperature rows have no symbol.
- The C~pd~ line, printed as a one-row box under the switching table
  ("25 pF typ"), is the table in Electrical characteristics.
- The SN74HC4002 columns are kept because the PDF covers them, though
  no SN74HC4002 part is claimed here; the package option addendum lists
