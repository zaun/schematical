# SN54F27, SN74F27 triple 3-input positive-NOR gates

## Description

These devices contain three independent 3-input NOR gates. They perform
the Boolean functions Y = !{A} • !{B} • !{C} or Y = !{A + B + C} in
positive logic. [[1::2]]

The SN54F27 is characterized for operation over the full military
temperature range of –55°C to 125°C. The SN74F27 is characterized for
operation from 0°C to 70°C. [[1::3]]
