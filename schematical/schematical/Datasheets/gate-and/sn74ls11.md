# SN54LS11, SN54S11, SN74LS11, SN74S11 triple 3-input positive-AND gates

## Description

These devices contain three independent 3-input AND gates. Each gate
performs Y = A · B · C or Y = !{!{A} + !{B} + !{C}} in positive logic.

The SN54LS11 and SN54S11 are characterized for operation over the full
military temperature range of –55 °C to 125 °C. The SN74LS11 and
SN74S11 are characterized for operation from 0 °C to 70 °C.
