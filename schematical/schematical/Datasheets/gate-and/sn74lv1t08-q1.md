# SN74LV1T08-Q1 automotive 2-input positive-AND gate with integrated translation

## Description

The SN74LV1T08-Q1 is a 2-input AND gate. Each gate performs the Boolean
function Y = A × B in positive logic. The output level is referenced to
the supply voltage (V~CC~) and supports 1.8-V, 2.5-V, 3.3-V, and 5-V
CMOS levels. It is AEC-Q100 qualified for automotive applications
(device temperature grade 1: -40°C to +125°C).

The input is designed with a lower threshold circuit to support up
translation for lower voltage CMOS inputs (for example, 1.2-V input to
1.8-V output or 1.8-V input to 3.3-V output). In addition, the 5-V
tolerant input pins enable down translation (for example, 3.3-V to
2.5-V output).

## Application information

### Outputs

Balanced CMOS push-pull outputs: the device can sink and source similar
currents. The drive capability of this device may create fast edges into
light loads, so routing and load conditions should be considered to
prevent ringing. The outputs are capable of driving larger currents than
the device can sustain without being damaged; the electrical and
thermal limits defined in the Absolute Maximum Ratings must be followed
at all times. Unused push-pull CMOS outputs should be left disconnected.

### LVxT enhanced input voltage

The SN74LV1T08-Q1 belongs to TI's LVxT family of logic devices with
integrated voltage level translation: reduced input voltage thresholds
support up-translation, and inputs tolerant of signals with up to 5.5 V
levels support down-translation. The output voltage will always be
referenced to the supply voltage (V~CC~). For proper functionality,
input signals must remain at or above the specified V~IH~(MIN) level for
a HIGH input state, and at or below the specified V~IL~(MAX) for a LOW
input state (see [Conversion notes](#conversion-notes)).

The inputs are high impedance and are typically modeled as a resistor in
parallel with the input capacitance. The inputs require that input
signals transition between valid logic states quickly, as defined by
the input transition rate in the
[recommended operating conditions](#recommended-operating-conditions).
Failing to meet this specification will result in excessive power
consumption and could cause oscillations. Do not leave inputs floating
at any time during operation. Unused inputs must be terminated at V~CC~
or GND. If a system will not be actively driving an input at all times,
then a pull-up or pull-down resistor can be added to provide a valid
input voltage during these times; a 10-kΩ resistor is recommended.

Down translation: ensure that the input signals in the HIGH state are
between V~IH~(MIN) and 5.5 V, and input signals in the LOW state are
lower than V~IL~(MAX). Up translation: standard CMOS inputs for a device
operating at a 5-V supply will have a V~IH~(MIN) of 3.5 V; for the
SN74LV1T08-Q1, V~IH~(MIN) with a 5-V supply is only 2 V, which would
allow for up-translation from a typical 2.5-V to 5-V signals.

| V~CC~ | Up translation: inputs from | Down translation: inputs from |
|---|---|---|
| 1.8-V | 1.2-V | 2.5-V, 3.3-V, and 5.0-V |
| 2.5-V | 1.8-V | 3.3-V and 5.0-V |
| 3.3-V | 1.8-V and 2.5-V | 5.0-V |
| 5.0-V | 2.5-V and 3.3-V | |

### Clamp diodes

The outputs have both positive and negative clamping diodes, and the
inputs have negative clamping diodes only. Voltages beyond the values
specified in the Absolute Maximum Ratings table can cause damage to the
device. The input and output voltage ratings may be exceeded if the
input and output clamp-current ratings are observed.

### Typical application

Three 2-input AND gates are combined to produce a 4-input AND gate
function; multiple SN74LV1T08-Q1 directly control the RESET pin of a
motor controller. The controller requires four input signals (!{OC},
PG, ON/!{OFF}, !{OT}) to all be HIGH before being enabled, and should be
disabled in the event that any one signal goes LOW; the 4-input AND
combines the four individual reset signals into a single active-low
reset signal.

Design requirements:

- The positive supply must be capable of sourcing current equal to the
  total current sourced by all outputs plus the maximum static supply
  current I~CC~ and any transient current required for switching; the
  ground must sink the matching total. Be sure to not exceed the maximum
  total current through V~CC~ or GND listed in the Absolute Maximum
  Ratings.
- The device can drive a load with a total capacitance less than or
  equal to 50 pF while still meeting all of the data sheet
  specifications. Larger capacitive loads can be applied; however, it
  is not recommended to exceed 50 pF.
- Resistive load: R~L~ ≥ V~O~ / I~O~, with V~O~ and I~O~ from V~OH~ and
  V~OL~ in the Electrical Characteristics; in the HIGH state, V~O~ is
  the difference between the output voltage and V~CC~.
- Input signals must cross V~IL~(max) to be considered a logic LOW, and
  V~IH~(min) to be considered a logic HIGH. Do not exceed the maximum
  input voltage range found in the Absolute Maximum Ratings.
- Push-pull outputs that could be in opposite states, even for a very
  short time period, should never be connected directly together. Two
  channels with the same input signals can be connected in parallel for
  additional output drive strength. Unused outputs can be left
  floating. Do not connect outputs directly to V~CC~ or ground.

Design procedure: add a decoupling capacitor from V~CC~ to GND, placed
physically close to the device and electrically close to both the
V~CC~ and GND pins. Ensure the capacitive load at the output is
≤ 50 pF. This is not a hard limit; it will, however, ensure optimal
performance. Ensure the resistive load at the output is larger than
(V~CC~ / I~O(max)~) Ω, so that the maximum output current from the
Absolute Maximum Ratings is not violated.

### Power and layout

The power supply can be any voltage between the minimum and maximum
supply voltage rating in the recommended operating conditions. Each
V~CC~ terminal should have a good bypass capacitor to prevent power
disturbance; a 0.1-μF capacitor is recommended. The 0.1-μF and 1-μF
capacitors are commonly used in parallel. The bypass capacitor should
be installed as close to the power terminal as possible.

Inputs must never be left floating. All unused inputs of digital logic
devices must be connected to a logic high or logic low voltage, as
defined by the input voltage specifications, to prevent them from
floating; generally, the inputs are tied to GND or V~CC~.
