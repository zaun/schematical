# SN54F11, SN74F11 triple 3-input positive-AND gates

## Description

These devices contain three independent 3-input AND gates. They
perform the Boolean functions Y = A • B • C or
Y = !{!{A} + !{B} + !{C}} in positive logic.

The SN54F11 is characterized for operation over the full military
temperature range of –55 °C to 125 °C. The SN74F11 is characterized for
operation from 0 °C to 70 °C.
