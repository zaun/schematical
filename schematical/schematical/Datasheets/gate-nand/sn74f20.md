# SN54F20, SN74F20 dual 4-input positive-NAND gates

## Description

These devices contain two independent 4-input NAND gates. They perform
the Boolean functions Y = !{A • B • C • D} or
Y = !{A} + !{B} + !{C} + !{D} in positive logic.

The SN54F20 is characterized for operation over the full military
temperature range of –55°C to 125°C. The SN74F20 is characterized for
operation from 0°C to 70°C.
