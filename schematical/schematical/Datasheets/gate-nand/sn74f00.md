# SN54F00, SN74F00 quadruple 2-input positive-NAND gates

## Description

These devices contain four independent 2-input NAND gates. They perform
the Boolean functions Y = !{A • B} or Y = !{A} + !{B} in positive logic. [[1::2]]

The SN54F00 is characterized for operation over the full military
temperature range of –55°C to 125°C. The SN74F00 is characterized for
operation from 0°C to 70°C. [[1::3]]
