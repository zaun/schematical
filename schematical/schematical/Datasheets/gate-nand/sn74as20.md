# SN54ALS20A, SN54AS20, SN74ALS20A, SN74AS20 dual 4-input positive-NAND gates

## Description

These devices contain two independent 4-input positive-NAND gates.
They perform the Boolean functions Y = !{A • B • C • D} or
Y = !{A} + !{B} + !{C} + !{D} in positive logic. [[1::2]]

The SN54ALS20A and SN54AS20 are characterized for operation over the
full military temperature range of −55°C to 125°C. The SN74ALS20A and
SN74AS20 are characterized for operation from 0°C to 70°C. [[1::3]]

## Parameter measurement information

Series 54ALS/74ALS and 54AS/74AS devices. C~L~ includes probe and jig
capacitance. All input pulses have the following characteristics:
PRR ≤ 1 MHz, t~r~ = t~f~ = 2 ns, duty cycle = 50%. The outputs are
measured one at a time with one transition per measurement. [[5::1]]

Conversion note: in the figure the input pulses swing from 0.3 V to 3.5 V and all waveforms are measured at 1.3 V.
