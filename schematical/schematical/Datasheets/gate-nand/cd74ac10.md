# CD74AC10 triple 3-input positive-NAND gates

## Description

The CD74AC10 contains three independent 3-input NAND gates. This device
performs the Boolean function Y = !{A • B • C} or Y = !{A} + !{B} + !{C}
in positive logic.

AC Types Feature 1.5-V to 5.5-V Operation and Balanced Noise Immunity
at 30% of the Supply Voltage. Speed of Bipolar F, AS, and S, With
Significantly Reduced Power Consumption. Balanced Propagation Delays.
±24-mA Output Drive Current. Fanout to 15 F Devices. SCR
Latchup-Resistant CMOS Process and Circuit Design. Exceeds 2-kV ESD
Protection Per MIL-STD-883, Method 3015.

## Parameter measurement information

C~L~ includes probe and test-fixture capacitance. All input pulses are
supplied by generators having the following characteristics:
PRR ≤ 1 MHz, Z~O~ = 50 Ω, t~r~ = 3 ns, t~f~ = 3 ns. Phase relationships
between waveforms are arbitrary. The outputs are measured one at a time
with one input transition per measurement. t~PLH~ and t~PHL~ are the
same as t~pd~.

Conversion note: the load circuit of Figure 1 also has R1 = 500 Ω from
the output to switch S1 (2 × V~CC~, Open or GND), and S1 is Open for
t~PLH~/t~PHL~, which leaves R2 and C~L~ below. When V~CC~ = 1.5 V,
R1 = R2 = 1 kΩ.
