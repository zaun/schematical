# SN54ALS10A, SN54AS10, SN74ALS10A, SN74AS10 triple 3-input positive-NAND gates

## Description

These devices contain three independent 3-input positive-NAND gates.
They perform the Boolean functions Y = !{A • B • C} or
Y = !{A} + !{B} + !{C} in positive logic. [[1::1]]

The SN54ALS10A and SN54AS10 are characterized for operation over the
full military temperature range of −55°C to 125°C. The SN74ALS10A and
SN74AS10 are characterized for operation from 0°C to 70°C. [[1::2]]

## Parameter measurement information

Series 54ALS/74ALS and 54AS/74AS devices. C~L~ includes probe and jig
capacitance. All input pulses have the following characteristics:
PRR ≤ 1 MHz, t~r~ = t~f~ = 2 ns, duty cycle = 50%. The outputs are
measured one at a time with one transition per measurement. [[5::1]]
