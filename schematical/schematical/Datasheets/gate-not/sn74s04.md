# SN5404, SN54LS04, SN54S04, SN7404, SN74LS04, SN74S04 hex inverters

## Description

These devices contain six independent inverters. [[1::2]]

## Pinout

```pinout pinout=p14
```

```pinout pinout=w14_5404
```

```pinout pinout=lccc20
```

Conversion note: the source (p.1) draws the first pinout for the SN5404 J package, the SN54LS04 and SN54S04 J or W package and the SN7404, SN74LS04 and SN74S04 plastic packages, the second for the SN5404 W package, and the third for the SN54LS04 and SN54S04 FK package.

## Pin functions

```pins
1A, 2A, 3A, 4A, 5A, 6A: Inverter input
1Y, 2Y, 3Y, 4Y, 5Y, 6Y: Inverter output
GND: Ground
VCC: Supply voltage
NC: No internal connection
```

## Parameter measurement information

### Series 54/74 and 54S/74S devices

Conversion note: the load circuit for 2-state totem-pole outputs of Figure 1 (p.8) connects the output under test to the test point, to C~L~ to ground, and through a diode to R~L~ from V~CC~ and to a chain of diodes to ground.

C~L~ includes probe and jig capacitance. All diodes are 1N3064 or
equivalent. All input pulses are supplied by generators having the
following characteristics: PRR ≤ 1 MHz, Z~O~ ≈ 50 Ω; t~r~ and t~f~ ≤
7 ns for Series 54/74 devices and t~r~ and t~f~ ≤ 2.5 ns for Series
54S/74S devices. The outputs are measured one at a time, with one input
transition per measurement. [[8::2]]

- Source pages 1–9 converted; the package option addendum (p.10–14),
  tape and reel, tube and mechanical pages and the notice (15–32)
  dropped, replaced by the generated Package and Ordering sections. The
  ordering lines are from the package option addendum (p.10–13).
- Dropped: the feature line, the ordering information table of p.2 (the
  ordering lines are from the package option addendum), the vendor's pin drawings (p.1),
  the schematics (p.4), and from Figures 1 and 2 (p.8–9) the load
  circuits for open-collector and 3-state outputs and the pulse
  duration, setup and hold and enable and disable waveforms with their
  notes C and D. The logic diagram and the 2-state load circuits are
  described in text.
- The source has no pin functions table; the pin descriptions are the
  converter's, with the source's "No internal connection" for NC.
- The source does not say which device a JM38510 or M38510 number is;
  the tables are marked for those parts by the catalog's family (00105
  the SN5404, 30003 the SN54LS04, 07003 the SN54S04).
- The electrical characteristics tables give symbols only, so they have
  no Parameter column. NOTE 3 of the recommended operating conditions is
  a table note under each of the three tables, as the source prints it.
- The package option addendum also lists JM38510/00105BDA,
  JM38510/30003B2A, JM38510/30003BDA, JM38510/30003SCA, SNJ54S04J and
  further SN7404, SN74LS04 and SN74S04 orderables, which this file
  doesn't claim.
- From images: overbar of Y = !{A} (logic diagram, p.3).
- Source issues:
  - Source error: "This are stress ratings only" in note † of the
    absolute maximum ratings (p.5); kept as printed.
