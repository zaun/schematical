# CD4009UB, CD4010B CMOS hex buffers/converters

## Description

High-voltage types (20-volt rating). Inverting type: CD4009UB.
Non-inverting type: CD4010B. [[1::1]]

CD4009UB and CD4010B hex buffer/converters may be used as CMOS to TTL
or DTL logic-level converters or CMOS high-sink-current drivers. The
CD4049UB and CD4050B are preferred hex buffer replacements for the
CD4009UB and CD4010B, respectively, in all applications except
multiplexers. For applications not requiring high sink current or
voltage conversion, the CD4069UB hex inverter is recommended. [[1::2]] [[1::3]]

100% tested for quiescent current at 20 V. Maximum input current of
1 µA at 18 V over full package-temperature range; 100 nA at 18 V and
25°C. 5-V, 10-V, and 15-V parametric ratings. [[1::5]]

The CD4009UB and CD4010B types are supplied in 16-lead hermetic
dual-in-line ceramic packages (F3A suffix), 16-lead dual-in-line
plastic packages (E suffix), 16-lead small-outline packages (M, M96,
MT, and NSR suffixes), and 16-lead thin shink small-outline packages
(PW and PWR suffixes). [[1::4]]

## Functional description

All inputs are protected by CMOS protection network. Conversion note:
the schematic diagram of the CD4009UB (Fig. 1, 1 of 6 identical
stages) shows the input, swinging between V~DD~ and GND, driving an
inverter between V~DD~ and V~SS~ and an output stage between V~CC~ and
V~SS~, whose output swings between V~CC~ and GND. Configuration: hex
COS/MOS to DTL or TTL converter (inverting). Wiring schedule: connect
V~CC~ to DTL or TTL supply. Connect V~DD~ to COS/MOS supply. [[1::Figure 1]]

## Conversion notes

- Source pages 1–4 converted (the Harris data sheet). Dropped: the
  package option addendum, tape and reel, tube and mechanical pages and
  the notice (5–20), replaced by the generated Package and Ordering
  sections. Ordering packing, temperature and marking columns are from
  the package option addendum (p.5).
- Pages 1–4 are scans; every value was read from the page images
  (100 dpi, the tables, terminal assignments and figures at 220 dpi)
  and checked there. Only the document line and the package paragraph
  (p.1) have a text layer.
- The data sheet also covers the non-inverting CD4010B, which this
  file does not claim; its rows of the tables are kept, and its
  schematic diagram (Fig. 2) and curves are dropped. The package option
  addendum also lists CD4009UBEE4 and CD4009UBF3A, which the catalog
  doesn't carry.
- Dropped: the applications list, the functional diagrams and
  terminal-assignment drawings (the pinout is generated; the buffer
  equations are in the function table), the chip photograph with its
  dimensions and pad layout (p.4), and the typical-characteristic
  curves (Figs. 3 to 19: voltage transfer characteristics, output low
  and high current characteristics, propagation delay time and
  transition time vs. load capacitance, dissipation characteristics),
  which the scan shows too coarsely to digitize to 2 %.
- The static electrical characteristics table prints one unit for
  I~OL~ and I~OH~ together, one for V~OL~ and V~OH~ and one for V~IL~
  and V~IH~, and the dynamic table one per group of rows; it is written
