# SN54AS1004A, SN74ALS1004, SN74AS1004A hex inverting drivers

## Description

′AS1004A Offer High Capacitive-Drive Capability. Driver Version of
′ALS04B and ′AS04.

These devices contain six independent inverting drivers. They perform
the Boolean function Y = !{A}.

The SN54AS1004A is characterized for operation over the full military
temperature range of −55°C to 125°C. The SN74ALS1004 and SN74AS1004A are
characterized for operation from 0°C to 70°C.
